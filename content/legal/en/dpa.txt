PREAMBLE

The Processor provides services to the Controller in accordance with the
Terms of Use for smiit Analytics (Main Agreement). In doing so, it processes
personal data on behalf of the Controller. This Agreement specifies the
Parties' rights and obligations under data protection law pursuant to
Art. 28 GDPR. It applies to all activities that are connected with the Main
Agreement and in which employees of the Processor or third parties engaged by
it come into contact with personal data of the Controller.

The Processor provides its service as a standardised service for a large
number of customers under uniform technical and organisational conditions.
Accordingly, this Agreement is designed as a uniform document and applies
with identical content to all customers. Art. 28(9) GDPR expressly permits
conclusion in an electronic format; a handwritten signature is not required.

ANNEX 1 — SUBJECT MATTER, NATURE AND PURPOSE OF THE PROCESSING
===============================================================================

1. SUBJECT MATTER OF THE PROCESSING

Provision and operation of the software-as-a-service application smiit
Analytics for the business analysis of data that the Controller releases from
its bexio company.


2. NATURE OF THE PROCESSING

  - Retrieval of the released data via the bexio interface (read-only)
  - Storage in tenant-separated data storage
  - Structuring, preparation and linking into an analysis model
  - Querying and presentation in reports, dashboards and exports
  - Analysis via an AI-assisted analysis function, if activated by the
    Controller
  - Logging of access and security-relevant events
  - Erasure in accordance with § 12 of this Agreement


3. PURPOSE OF THE PROCESSING

Provision of the services agreed in the Main Agreement, in particular the
provision of business analyses for the Controller. The Processor does not
process for its own purposes the data from the bexio connection or any content
that the Controller and its Users enter in the application. § 1(7) applies to
the account and usage data of the Users.


4. CATEGORIES OF DATA SUBJECTS

  a) Users of the application (employees and agents of the Controller)
  b) Employees of the Controller whose data from bexio is analysed
  c) Customers of the Controller and their contact persons
  d) Suppliers of the Controller and their contact persons
  e) Other business contacts of the Controller recorded in bexio


5. TYPES OF PERSONAL DATA

5.1 Data of the Users of the application

  - Content of requests to the AI-assisted analysis function
  - Logs of access to and changes to Customer data

The account and usage data of the Users (name, e-mail address, password as a
hash value, role, language and time zone settings, details of two-factor
authentication and of trusted devices, login logs and details of the use of
the functions) is processed by the Processor as a controller in its own right;
it is not the subject matter of this Agreement (§ 1(7)).

5.2 Data from the bexio connection

The bexio authorisation is granted uniformly for the entire scope described
below; no selection of individual data areas by the Controller is provided for
(§ 3(3)). The categories actually retrieved are those that are available in
the Controller's bexio company according to its modules and permissions.

  a) Contact data
     Name, salutation, title, address, communication data, language, country,
     assignment to contact groups and industries

  b) Document data
     Invoices, quotes, orders, delivery notes, line items, payments,
     reminders, subscriptions and the persons assigned to each of them

  c) Purchasing data
     Supplier invoices, expenses, outgoing payments, purchase orders and the
     persons assigned to each of them

  d) Accounting data
     Chart of accounts, journal entries, tax rates, financial and calendar
     years, VAT periods

  e) Bank data
     Bank accounts including IBAN, payments including payee and payer
     details

  f) Item and inventory data
     Item master data, prices, stock levels, storage locations

  g) Project and time data
     Projects, milestones, work packages and RECORDED WORKING HOURS PER
     PERSON including billability, activity and project assignment

  h) Personnel data ("Payroll" area)
     Master data of the employees including address, holiday and hours
     balances as at cut-off dates and ABSENCES INCLUDING THE REASON FOR
     ABSENCE RECORDED IN bexio

  i) Other data
     bexio users, company profile, notes and tasks from customer management
     (free-text content), lookup values

  j) Data stored by the Controller itself in the
     application
     internal cost rates and billing rates per person or service, budgets,
     target working hours, key figure definitions


6. SPECIAL CATEGORIES OF PERSONAL DATA (ARTICLE 9 GDPR)

The connection includes the "Payroll" data area and thus the reasons for
absence under Section 5.2(h). Depending on how the Controller records them in
bexio, these may allow conclusions to be drawn about the state of health of
individual employees and thus constitute health data within the meaning of
Art. 9(1) GDPR.

As no selection of individual data areas is provided for (§ 3(3)), this
processing cannot be deselected when using the application. It is omitted only
if the Controller does not grant the bexio authorisation at all or if its
bexio company does not include the "Payroll" area.

The Processor processes this data exclusively within the scope of the general
analysis functions; no separate analysis by health characteristics takes
place. The Controller is responsible for the existence of a legal basis under
Art. 9(2) GDPR (§ 3(2)(a)).


7. DATA RELATING TO WORK PERFORMANCE AND WORK BEHAVIOUR

The data categories under Section 5.2(g) and (h) as well as (j) enable
analyses of the working time, the utilisation and the billability of
individual employees. The Controller has been informed that this may give rise
to co-determination rights of an employee representative body (§ 3(2)(d)).


8. DURATION OF THE PROCESSING

For the term of the Main Agreement plus the periods under § 12 of this
Agreement. Within the term of the Agreement, the following automated erasure
periods apply:

  - Conversation histories of the AI-assisted analysis function: 90 days after
    the last activity
  - Unmodified raw data from the bexio interface (data minimisation): after
    30 days; the analysis data prepared from it is retained for the duration
    of the connection
  - Logs of the synchronisation runs: after 90 days


9. AI-ASSISTED ANALYSIS FUNCTION: SHARING LEVELS

The AI-assisted analysis function is switched off by default. Only a person
with the "Owner" role can switch it on, and only after confirming a notice on
the data transferred; this confirmation is documented with the time, the
person and the version of the notice and is deemed an instruction under
§ 2(4).

In the default setting ("Standard"), names and e-mail addresses of persons
are replaced by placeholders before the transfer to the model provider, and
free texts are not transferred; company names are not pseudonymised. The
Controller can lift these restrictions step by step, each time with a separate
confirmation:

  a) "Names": names of contacts (customers, suppliers and their contact
     persons) in plain text;
  b) "Names + texts": additionally free texts (posting, line item and time
     recording texts, document titles, comments);
  c) "Everything": no pseudonymisation, not even for employees, bexio users,
     members of the workspace and e-mail addresses.

Personnel data under Section 5.2(h) is only transferred if the Controller has
released it separately. Switching off the function resets all releases.

ANNEX 2 — TECHNICAL AND ORGANISATIONAL MEASURES (ARTICLE 32 GDPR)
===============================================================================

Last updated: 18 November 2026


1. CONFIDENTIALITY

1.1 Physical access control
    Operations take place exclusively in data centres of the sub-processor
    Microsoft (Microsoft Azure). No own data centres or server rooms are
    operated. The physical security measures are set out in the
    certifications of the sub-processor (Annex 3).

1.2 System access control
    - Login exclusively via personal user accounts
    - Passwords with a minimum length of ten characters
    - Passwords stored exclusively as Argon2 hash values
    - Mandatory two-factor authentication for all Users by one-time e-mail
      code (valid for 10 minutes, a maximum of five attempts); trusted
      devices for a maximum of 30 days
    - Short-lived access tokens (15 minutes); sessions end no later than
      7 days after login, regardless of activity; overview of the active
      sessions and sign-out on all devices by the User
    - Automatic account lockout for 15 minutes after five failed attempts
    - Mandatory confirmation of the e-mail address before first use
    - Session cookies exclusively with the attributes "Secure", "HttpOnly"
      and the "__Host" prefix
    - Protection against automated login attempts through rate limiting at the
      level of the upstream web server
    - Locked accounts respond as with an incorrect password, so that the
      existence of an account cannot be determined

1.3 Data access control
    - Role-based authorisation concept with the roles Owner, Admin and
      Member
    - Tenant separation at database level through row-based security
      policies (Row Level Security) with enforced application
      (FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY)
    - The application connects to the database exclusively with database
      roles without superuser rights, so that the security policies cannot be
      circumvented
    - Separate database roles by task: one role for application operation, a
      read-only role for the analysis layer, a read-only role for the
      AI-assisted analysis function
    - The AI-assisted analysis function only has read access to the analysis
      schema; access to raw data, credentials or system tables is technically
      excluded
    - Every database query generated by the AI is checked by an upstream query
      filter
    - Limitation of the execution time and the result size of queries
    - Platform administrators of the Processor have no access to reports,
      conversation histories of the AI-assisted analysis function or data from
      the bexio connection; this is technically enforced by the access rules
      of the database
    - Support access to a workspace only with the consent of a person with the
      "Owner" role, limited to a maximum of 30 days, revocable at any time and
      visible in the member list; no logging in "as a User"
    - Access to the administration area only with two-factor verification
      that is valid for a maximum of four hours
    - Logging of every action of the platform administrators in the audit
      log

1.4 Separation control
    - Logical tenant separation of all data holdings based on a unique
      workspace identifier
    - Enforcement of the separation at database level, not solely in the
      application logic
    - Automated tests demonstrating tenant separation

1.5 Pseudonymisation and encryption
    - Transmission exclusively via TLS (HTTPS); unencrypted access attempts
      are redirected
    - Encryption of data at rest by the platform encryption of the
      sub-processor
    - Encryption of the bexio access tokens at rest with a separate key
    - Pseudonymisation of names and e-mail addresses before the transfer to
      the model provider (Annex 1, Section 9)
    - Removal of access and security credentials from the addresses in the
      server logs; erasure of the server logs after 30 days
    - No storage of IP addresses in the telemetry (Application Insights)


2. INTEGRITY

2.1 Input control
    - Audit-proof, append-only logging of security-relevant and
      data-modifying operations with timestamp and user ID

2.2 Transfer control
    - No transfer of data carriers
    - Transfer to sub-processors exclusively via encrypted connections
    - Restrictive Content Security Policy to prevent content from leaking via
      embedded resources
    - Neutralisation of formula cells in data exports to prevent formula
      injection


3. AVAILABILITY AND RESILIENCE

    - Automated backup of the database by the sub-processor with a retention
      period of 35 days and the option of restoring to a specific point in
      time
    - Geo-redundant storage of the backup copies in a second Swiss Azure
      region (Switzerland West)
    - Deletion protection on the database and the key management against
      accidental deletion
    - Monitoring of operational readiness via status endpoints
    - Limitation of concurrent database connections to protect against
      overload
    - Rate limiting of security-critical and resource-intensive functions
    - Resumption of interrupted processing runs without data loss


4. PROCEDURE FOR REGULAR REVIEW

4.1 Review
    - Regular security reviews of the source code and system architecture with
      documented results
    - Automated checking of the third-party libraries used for known
      vulnerabilities in the build process
    - Pinning of the container base images by cryptographic checksums
    - Automated tests as evidence of the effectiveness of tenant separation
      and of the access restrictions

4.2 Control of processing on instructions
    - Written agreements with all sub-processors
    - Commitment of all employees to confidentiality
    - Documented procedure for reporting personal data breaches

4.3 Data protection by default
    - The AI-assisted analysis function is switched off by default and is
      only switched on by an Owner; names and e-mail addresses are
      pseudonymised by default, personnel data is only transferred after
      separate release (Annex 1, Section 9)
    - The bexio connection is exclusively read-only; write access to the bexio
      company is not technically provided for
    - The Controller can revoke the bexio authorisation at any time; data
      retrieval then ceases
    - The Controller can delete individual datasets or its entire workspace
      itself; the data is automatically erased within four hours
    - Automated erasure of the conversation histories of the AI-assisted
      analysis function 90 days after the last activity

    Note: A selection of individual bexio data areas by the Controller is
    currently not provided for (§ 3(3)).
