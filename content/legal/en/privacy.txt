4.9 AI-ASSISTED ANALYSIS FEATURE

The application contains an analysis feature based on a large language model.
You can ask questions about your data in natural language. You are informed in
the application that the answers are generated by an AI system.

The feature is switched off by default. Only an owner of the workspace can
switch it on, and only after confirming a notice stating which data are
transmitted to the provider of the language model. As long as it is switched
off, no transmission takes place.

  Data:                 your question, the conversation history, a description
                        of the available data structure and the results of the
                        database queries generated for this purpose (key
                        figures and limited extracts of data rows)
  Purpose:              answering your question
  Legal basis:          Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; insofar as data from the Customer's
                        bexio company are concerned, we process them on behalf
                        of the Customer (see below)
  Recipients:           OpenAI as Processor (see Section 5)
  Place of processing:  European Economic Area and Switzerland; contracting
                        party in Ireland, European endpoint
                        https://eu.api.openai.com/v1 (see Sections 5 and 6)
  Retention period:     conversation histories are automatically deleted on our
                        side 90 days after the last activity; for the retention
                        period at the model provider, see Section 5

PSEUDONYMISATION. Before transmission, we replace names and e-mail addresses of
persons with placeholders (such as "Person-XXXXX"); free texts such as posting
texts and line item texts are not transmitted. The owner of the workspace can
lift these restrictions in stages, in each case after confirming a separate
notice:

  a) "Names": names of contacts (customers, suppliers and their contact
     persons) are transmitted in plain text;
  b) "Names + texts": free texts in addition;
  c) "Everything": no pseudonymisation, not even for employees, bexio users,
     members of the workspace and e-mail addresses.

Company names are not pseudonymised. Personnel data (employees, absences,
holiday and time balances) only reach the language model if the owner has
specifically released them.

The transmitted content is NOT used to train language models. The model
provider does not store the requests for later reuse. The processing takes
place in the European Economic Area and in Switzerland; details, including on
the logs for detecting abuse, are described in Section 6.

Insofar as the content processed concerns data from our Customer's bexio
company, we act as that Customer's Processor; to that extent, the Customer is
the Controller (see Section 3).

6. TRANSFERS TO THIRD COUNTRIES

We operate our application and our database in Microsoft data centres in
Switzerland. From the perspective of the GDPR, Switzerland is a third country
for which the European Commission has adopted an adequacy decision (Art. 45
GDPR). Additional safeguards such as standard contractual clauses (SCCs) are
therefore not required for the processing in Switzerland. The same applies to
the sending of e-mails.

Microsoft stores feedback in Azure DevOps in the "North Europe" region
(Ireland) and thus within the European Union; no additional safeguards are
required for this. Insofar as Microsoft processes data outside Switzerland and
the European Economic Area in connection with Azure Front Door or Azure
DevOps, this is based on the standard contractual clauses of the European
Commission (Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR), which form part of Microsoft's data protection
terms, and on the certification of Microsoft Corporation under the EU-US Data
Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

When our website is accessed, GitHub, Inc. processes your IP address in the
United States of America (Section 4.1). GitHub is certified under the EU-US
Data Privacy Framework; the transfer is based on the European Commission's
adequacy decision in this respect (Art. 45 GDPR). For persons in Switzerland,
the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework applies accordingly, which Switzerland
has recognised as adequate for certified companies.

PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A. has its registered office in Luxembourg
and may transfer data to other companies of the PayPal group, including to
third countries such as the United States of America. PayPal bases these
transfers on binding corporate rules (Binding Corporate Rules, Art. 47 GDPR);
details in PayPal's privacy statement (Section 5).

For the AI-assisted analysis feature (Section 4.9), we transmit the content of
a request to our Processor OpenAI Ireland Limited, which has its registered
office in Ireland. We use exclusively the European endpoint
https://eu.api.openai.com/v1 with the data residency region Europe (Section 5).
The requests are therefore processed within the European Economic Area and
Switzerland. No transfer to a third country takes place in this respect: the
European Economic Area is subject to the GDPR, and there is an adequacy
decision of the European Commission for Switzerland (Art. 45 GDPR). OpenAI does
not commit to a storage location for the logs for the detection of abuse, which
OpenAI retains for up to 30 days. Insofar as OpenAI processes these logs or, in
individual cases, other data outside the European Economic Area and
Switzerland, the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission apply
(Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR, Implementing Decision (EU) 2021/914), which form part of
the OpenAI Data Processing Addendum (Section 4.1 of the Addendum).

The transfer to OpenAI only takes place if the AI-assisted analysis feature is
switched on and used in the respective workspace. Names and e-mail addresses of
persons are pseudonymised by default (Section 4.9). The transmitted content is
not used to train models.

The following applies to our Customers in Switzerland: since our application
and our database are operated in Switzerland, there is no disclosure abroad in
this respect. The disclosure to OpenAI in the context of the AI-assisted
analysis feature is permitted because the member states of the European Union
as well as Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein are listed in Annex 1 to the Data
Protection Ordinance (DPO) as states with adequate data protection (Art. 16(1)
of the Federal Act on Data Protection, FADP); insofar as OpenAI processes the
data in Switzerland, there is no disclosure abroad. The OpenAI Data Processing
Addendum expressly also covers data that are subject to Swiss data protection
law.

ACCESS BY US AUTHORITIES. OpenAI Ireland Limited belongs to the OpenAI group of
companies headquartered in the United States of America. Data residency
determines where the data are processed and stored; it does not rule out that
US authorities, on the basis of the US CLOUD Act (Clarifying Lawful Overseas
Use of Data Act, 18 U.S.C. § 2713), require a US company to hand over data that
it controls, even if these data are stored outside the USA. Such a demand
generally requires a court order or a comparable order in the context of
criminal proceedings concerning serious crimes, such as terrorism, organised
crime or serious fraud, and is targeted at specific persons or accounts; the
CLOUD Act does not permit indiscriminate mass queries. The provider can
challenge such an order if it conflicts with the law of the country in which
the data are located. For the business data processed by us, such access is
therefore a rare exception. In addition, we have taken technical measures to
ensure that OpenAI receives as little data as possible: the feature is switched
off by default, names and e-mail addresses are pseudonymised and requests are
not stored for reuse (Section 4.9).
